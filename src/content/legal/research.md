## What you are agreeing to

If you turn on **Research Data Consent**, you allow syrup to use the **content** of your conversations with the agent to improve syrup and for research.

**Content** means: your prompts, the agent's replies, the tools it called and their results, the files it read or changed as part of a session, and signals about the outcome, such as whether you accepted, edited or reverted the agent's changes.

## What we do with it

- Study where the agent fails and why, and improve prompts, routing between models, and the tools.
- Build datasets to evaluate models and agent behavior.
- Publish **aggregate** findings (for example, "model X failed on 12% of edit tasks"). We never publish your code, your prompts, or anything that identifies you.

## What we do first

Before content is used for research we run automated redaction for API keys and other secrets, email addresses, and IP-like strings. Research datasets are kept separately from your live account data and are accessible only to people working on syrup under confidentiality obligations.

## What this does not include

- Your provider API keys. Never.
- Your account details beyond an internal, non-reversible identifier.
- Selling your data or sharing it with advertisers.

## Off by default, and never required

This consent is **off** until you turn it on. Using syrup does not depend on it. You can switch it off at any time in **Settings → Privacy**. From that moment we stop using new content, and we remove your existing content from research datasets we control within 30 days. Aggregate findings already published cannot be un-published, but they contain nothing that identifies you.

## Legal basis and duration

Your explicit consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and, where applicable, Article 9 does not apply because we do not seek special-category data). Consent lasts until you withdraw it or delete your account. We record the version of this text you agreed to and when.
